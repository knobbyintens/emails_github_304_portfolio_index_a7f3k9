The planner decomposes a delivery instance into a classical pre-processing stage and a quadratic-unconstrained-binary-optimisation core. A penalty formulation encodes vehicle capacity and delivery windows, while a local search repair step handles infeasible samples. The write-up reports reductions in total travel distance on synthetic instances modelled after city distribution networks.
