Tiny Model Zoo standardises data splits, tokenisers and scoring scripts so that results for sub-100M parameter models can be compared directly. The write-up argues that most reported gains in this regime are sensitive to tokeniser choice and evaluation prompt format, and proposes a minimum reporting checklist for future work.
